# Learner AI — World Ready Enterprise Project

**Learner AI** is an interactive, method-first educational assistant built for my **World Ready** school enterprise showcase.

Unlike conventional AI chatbots that simply hand students the final answer, **Learner AI** uses a guided, Socratic learning approach. It breaks problems down into structured steps, highlights common misconceptions, and coaches learners through *how* to solve problems independently.

---

## 🎯 Enterprise Vision & Problem Solved
Many students rely on standard AI tools as shortcuts, copying final answers without understanding the underlying method. **Learner AI** bridges that gap by acting as a digital study coach—building genuine problem-solving skills, critical thinking, and exam confidence across core British-curriculum subjects.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Method Over Straight Answers**: Guides students step-by-step through problem-solving frameworks (such as the balancing method in Maths or P.E.E. paragraphs in English) without spoiling the solution.
- **Misconception & Trap Detection**: Recognises common student mistakes—such as confusing a simile with a metaphor or mixing up WWI and WWII dates—and explains *why* an approach needs adjusting.
- **8 British-Curriculum Subjects**: Supports **Maths**, **English**, **Biology**, **Chemistry**, **Physics**, **Geography**, **History**, and **Computer Science**.
- **Interactive Hint System**: Includes a one-click **💡 Hint** button that gives targeted nudges whenever a learner gets stuck on a step.
- **Zero-Latency Showcase Mode**: Engineered as a lightweight, self-contained web app (`HTML`, `CSS`, and `JavaScript`) so it runs reliably at live showcase stalls without API delays or sign-in barriers.
